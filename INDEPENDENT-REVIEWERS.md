# Invitation: independent research-method assessment (voluntary)

ArcTopia invites **two independent volunteer reviewers** to assess a small, blinded set of research-method vignettes for a separate, private research study on claim warrant and research-process history.

**This is not a request to validate ArcTopia, endorse its theories, or contribute code.** The first-stage question is whether independent people can apply a specified six-level assessment rubric consistently. The results may support or challenge the method.

## What participation involves

- Independently review **16 short, blinded cases** and assign a level from **L0–L5** with a brief reason for each.
- Follow the provided instructions; do not collaborate with the other reviewer or use AI to assign ratings.
- Give feedback on unclear instructions separately from the ratings.
- Estimated time: **approximately 60–120 minutes**, to be confirmed after a non-study dry run. Participation is **unpaid and voluntary**.
- Prior research-methods, evidence-assessment, peer-review, metascience, or adjacent experience is useful. Reviewers must not have designed or previously seen the locked cases.

## Independence and privacy

The case packet will be supplied privately after eligibility and consent are confirmed; **the locked cases, pairing key, expected outcomes and individual ratings will not be posted in this public repository**. Participants may withdraw before their ratings are combined; data handling, attribution preference, retention and any later publication must be agreed before sharing material. No promise of full anonymity is made.

## How to express interest

Contact **linda@arctopia.no** with subject **Independent reviewer – Claim Warrant Study 1**. Briefly state relevant experience, any prior involvement with ArcTopia or the case materials, and whether the estimated time commitment is feasible. Please do not send sensitive personal information.

The study owner will check independence and provide the full information sheet and rubric before any participation. Expressions of interest are not enrollment.

## Scientific status

The locked Study 1 human-review gate has **not** been completed. Separate synthetic engineering tests do **not** replace independent human ratings and are not evidence of external validity. This public invitation does not disclose or modify the private study protocol.

**Research ownership:** ArcTopia research concepts and methods are Linda Thorstensen's original work. Participation as a reviewer does not transfer ownership or imply co-authorship or co-invention.
