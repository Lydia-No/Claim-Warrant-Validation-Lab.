# Why the public lab cannot yet answer its central question

**Decision note — 29 October 2026**

The formula-revision experiment was evaluated and found insufficient for the central hypothesis because its structured records do not contain independently established evidence histories.

The three historical evidence-chain dossiers are a **different kind of progress**: real, source-linked histories with explicit rival explanations (sampling/weighting, dust foregrounds, synchronization faults). They demonstrate *candidate inputs with meaningful evidential changes*, but they are neither novel phenomena nor a validated measurement.

## Unresolved barriers

1. **Historical hindsight:** all cases are famous corrections or reinterpretations; their later outcomes are already known.
2. **Non-independent curation:** the dossiers were authored by an assistant from existing source descriptions, not by two independently rating domain specialists.
3. **Publication ≠ proof:** a source link verifies that an account exists; it does not automatically prove causal or empirical correctness, nor does the source chain pass a historical epistemic entitlement threshold.
4. **No identical terminal evidence matched pairs:** each sequence has substantively different evidence and claims at later dates. They are *not* direct tests of equal terminal observables / differing provenance.
5. **No fair generic critic comparison:** no generic critic was run. Its design requires both versions, comparable evidence, and a stable question; the public lab's earlier snapshot baseline was intentionally weaker.
6. **Two incompatible outcome concepts:** "warrants rechecking" is not equivalent to "highest justified scientific claim." Do not compare or aggregate without a defined construct.
7. **Data and license constraints:** reproduced PDFs, original datasets, permissions for private corpora and specialist validation remain separate work.

## Explicit testable next question

Can an independent adjudicator, given a *fixed claim and an evidence-history record*, identify a specific support condition that remains open after a purported correction, **where** the same generic adjudicator given the full history would not detect it under matched effort and evidence?

A claim of added value requires performance on **new, unseen, independently rated cases**, under complete information parity. Null outcomes or excess false alarms are permissible—and must be reported.

The three historical dossiers are for refining what records must contain, not training the existing classifier against public correction stories and proclaiming improved accuracy.

**Go/no-go:** No go for comparative effectiveness or originality claims until sources, pair sampling and independent ratings are all established. Go for transparent evidence-graph schema checking, documentary provenance and study feasibility.
