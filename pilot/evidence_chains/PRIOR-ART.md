# Prior-art and originality check for historical evidence-chain records

**Scope:** Public evidence-chain pilot, 29 October 2026. This note is *not* an exhaustive novelty search or peer-reviewed originality determination.

## Clearly established prior art

**W3C PROV-O (2013):** https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/

A mature standard represents entities, activities, agents, provenance, derivations, revisions, source attribution and time constraints. The mere ability to build a chronological graph of sources, revisions or used evidence is not new.

**Nanopublications:** https://nanopub.net/

Small, machine-readable scientific assertions can already be packaged with explicit provenance and publication metadata. Claim–provenance linkage, structured evidence-attribution, and citable scientific micro-assertions are not inherently original features.

**Scientific correction and replication workflows:** The three cases in this folder are well-known episodes of corrections, alternative explanations and subsequent measurements. They document common scientific practice, not a newly discovered causal phenomenon.

## What the current implementation actually adds

- A modest, public, source-linked, chronological **case file format** for change-of-warrant research, with testable integrity constraints.
- A toy Python check that rejects time travel (citation to evidence dated later than the stage), unknown source IDs, duplicated nodes and missing residual-question descriptions.
- Explicit refusal to promote source existence into scientific claim verification.
- A reusable, transparent record of *which additional evidence would be needed to settle a source-level interpretive question*.

These are useful engineering functions. They are **not evidence of scientific novelty**.

## Narrow possible research remainder (still unproven)

A claim that could be novel would need a falsifiable rule that distinguishes two histories *where final observable evidence is held fixed* but the evidence-used provenance and unaddressed alternative conditions differ, and that **beats an equally informed generic critic** under independent specialist ratings *without excessive underclaiming or false alarms*.

Nothing in the current historical cases constitutes such a controlled same-terminal-evidence pair. The separate private Research Claim Governance Study 1 has its own locked protocol and its study materials must not be imported into this public repository. Do not use these examples to claim the private study passed, or to imply a new theory of provenance graphs.

## No-go criteria for novelty claims

Do not claim novelty for the evidence graph itself, for time-stamped citations, for correction history, or for detecting known errors from PR titles. Stop making superiority claims if an equally informed generic critic performs similarly or better; label this null finding as informative.

The next distinct research advance, if any, depends on independent review of permitted underlying evidence and a fair, previously unseen comparison with specified controls, while preserving an explicit overclaim/underclaim tradeoff.
