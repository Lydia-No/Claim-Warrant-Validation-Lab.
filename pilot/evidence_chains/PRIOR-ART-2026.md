# Originality gate — 29 October 2026

**Status: literature triage, not a comprehensive originality opinion. No comparative study has been run.**

The feasibility effort must not claim that linking evidence through time, attributing scientific sources, or making claims auditable are themselves new. Existing literature is already substantial.

## Directly overlapping prior art (verified public research records)

1. **Gelman & Loken, garden of forking paths.** Long-established explanation that the process of choosing analyses can change evidential interpretation even when only one final analysis is reported. Public discussion and working paper: https://stat.columbia.edu/~gelman/research/unpublished/p_hacking.pdf
2. **The Agentic Garden of Forking Paths (Miao, Pritchard & Zou, July 2026).** AI agents can produce different claims from the same dataset through defensible analytical choices; the authors propose the agentic-bootstrap / multiverse `m-value` approach. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01507
3. **Scientist-One: Verifiable Autonomous Research via Chain-of-Evidence (Google Research, 2026).** Claim-to-source evidence chains and auditing of phantom citations and method/code mismatches are existing research. https://research.google/pubs/scientist-one-verifiable-autonomous-research-via-chain-of-evidence/
4. **DeepTRACE (ICLR 2026).** End-to-end citation and claim-support auditing, with quantitative statement/citation matrices and human-calibrated judgment, already exists. https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/ad08767706825033b99122332293033d-Abstract-Conference.html
5. **NumTrace (Zhu et al., posted 26 October 2026).** Distinguishes correct numerical values from incorrect source binding, concept, period or unit, and evaluates support separately on real SEC filings. Thus "same visible value, different evidential meaning" is **not new on its own**. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34887
6. **W3C PROV-O.** Entity/activity/agent provenance, revision and derivation tracking has a well-established standard ontology. https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/

These sources have different scopes and varying evidential strength; e.g., a 2026 preprint is not automatically a replicated result. The relevance is to narrow *claimable originality*, not to endorse every finding.

## What the existing public lab does NOT establish

- That histories with identical final evidence but different provenance necessarily yield different justified levels of scientific claim.
- That its rules outperform a generic critic who receives all of the same evidence.
- That its current 3 historical correction narratives form matched terminal-evidence pairs (they do not).
- That bibliographic/source-metadata checks prove causal, mathematical, or operational mechanisms.
- That it has demonstrated a new category of audit failure not already anticipated by selective inference, multiverse analysis, provenance graph standards or chain-of-evidence audits.

## Remaining research opportunity (not a novelty claim)

A genuinely discriminating test would need to define a *specific* residual error not already captured by selective-inference or claim-binding methods, find independently auditable evidence histories that differ in that respect while holding relevant terminal observations fixed, and test **incremental error detection** against both a full-history generic critic and an appropriately chosen existing method. Measure false alarms and unreasonable claim suppression as well as missed unsupported claims.

Until then, the correct conclusion is **research question open; originality undemonstrated**. The present repository is a generic public feasibility sandbox and does not expose private foundational research protocols.

## Immediate decision

Use the existing historical dossiers to *discover* required source fields and limitations only. Do not tune the structural checker on these cases and report this as a blind advantage. Do not create fabricated counterfactual provenance for real-world cases and call it empirical evidence.
